# Portfolio vocabulary

- **Profile Card**: the code-rendered personal overview returned for a broad introduction. Portrait, schools, rankings, interests, and experience are owned by the application; the model supplies a short introduction.
- **Contact Card**: the code-rendered contact methods returned for a contact request. Email, phone, GitHub, LinkedIn, and WeChat are owned by the application and sourced from Hongxiang; the model selects the card. The visible card contains its title and contact methods without a separate lead-in.
- **Quick questions**: the same five prompts on the homepage and above the chat composer. All five broad presets return code-rendered cards selected by the AI. Fun Facts starts with an AI summary of public personality and interests plus compact destinations; TopGym expands only when selected. Focused questions receive grounded text. Each preset first calls the AI agent. After a successful reply, repeat clicks immediately focus that existing answer without another answer request, answer entrance animation, duplicate turn, or additional question quota. Suggested questions refresh independently in the background. Preset labels used in URLs share the same cache as the full prompts.
- **Suggested questions**: two short AI-generated follow-ups above Quick questions, based on the selected Me, Projects, Skills, or Fun Facts section, approved facts, and recent conversation. They enter with a restrained upward fade and a 75ms stagger; reduced motion shows them immediately. Clicking one sends an ordinary question. New ideas and repeated presets request new suggestions without delaying a cached answer or changing the draft. The separate `/api/followups` endpoint makes one bounded model call per generation, excluding the last 12 displayed questions and recently asked questions; there is no static question-bank fallback. Requests are debounced, cancelled on navigation/context changes, and protected against late results. Contact, New chat, and the 15-question limit hide suggestions. Failures leave the conversation usable; suggestions do not consume conversation turns. Suggestions use approved code-owned profile data, not a live vector database; nationality, visa, and sponsorship information has not been supplied.
- **Answer reveal**: a frontend visual animation applied after the complete API response arrives, not model streaming. Text remains available to selection and assistive technology; reduced-motion preferences show it immediately.
- **Conversation session**: completed turns, successful AI preset markers, and the current draft retained in sessionStorage for the browser tab. Returning through an already answered preset resumes its answer and preserves the draft; an unanswered preset calls the AI. Reload retains the cache; New chat clears it. Failed or interrupted requests never become cached answers. Restored cards use current approved data and skip entrance animations. Old saved cards without an AI preset marker remain readable but are not treated as AI cache entries.
- **Shared profile**: resume facts plus Hongxiang's supplied public interests, self-described ENTP/curiosity, fitness records, LeetCode notes link, Whatnot collaboration and contact details. Public interests include travel, skydiving, skiing, gaming and spending time with friends; no destinations, skill levels or achievements are inferred. MBTI is self-description, not a scientific assessment. The Notion link is supplied by Hongxiang; its page contents and visitor permissions have not been verified. An interest in SaaS companies does not imply business ownership.
- **Portfolio voice**: a first-person AI representation that can discuss general topics while grounding personal claims in the shared profile. It is not a live human chat.
- **Fun Facts Card**: the AI's returned summary appears above TopGym, LeetCode notes, Whatnot and Footprints. TopGym expands inline; LeetCode opens the supplied Notion link. Whatnot and travel Footprints are disabled Coming soon destinations, with no invented account or map. The summary is cached after a successful AI reply. `FUN_FACTS_REVISION` invalidates only outdated Fun Facts summaries when the public profile changes: old text remains readable, and the next preset click generates a new summary once.
- **TopGym**: the 2D pixel mini-game inside the optional Fun Facts expansion, also playable at `/gym` without a model call. Its primary heading is THE BIG THREE / 560 kg; old Kirby's Gym branding, tagline, footer and visible operation instructions were removed. Standard five-point mint/gold stars indicate reps. Choose squat, bench press, or deadlift; hold mouse, touch, Space, or Enter and release at 65–90% power. Three successful reps complete a set; switching lifts preserves each lift's count while mounted, while Rest cancels an unfinished lift. Collapsing or reloading resets the game. Page blur and cancelled touches cannot award a rep. Cached answers skip their entrance animation but preserve live controls and game animation; operation guidance remains available to screen readers.
