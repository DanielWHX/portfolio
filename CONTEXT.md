# Portfolio vocabulary

- **Profile Card**: the code-rendered personal overview returned for a broad introduction. Portrait, schools, rankings, interests, and experience are owned by the application; the model supplies a short introduction.
- **Contact Card**: the code-rendered contact methods returned for a contact request. Email, phone, GitHub, LinkedIn, and WeChat are owned by the application and sourced from Hongxiang; the model selects the card. The visible card contains its title and contact methods without a separate lead-in.
- **Quick questions**: the same five prompts on the homepage and above the chat composer. All five broad presets return code-rendered cards selected by the AI. Fun Facts starts with an AI summary of public personality and interests plus compact destinations; Big Three Record expands only when selected. Focused questions receive grounded text. Each preset first calls the AI agent. After a successful reply, repeat clicks immediately focus that existing answer without another answer request, answer entrance animation, duplicate turn, or additional question quota. Suggested questions refresh independently in the background. Preset labels used in URLs share the same cache as the full prompts.
- **Suggested questions**: two short AI-generated follow-ups above Quick questions, based on the selected Me, Projects, Skills, or Fun Facts section, approved facts, and recent conversation. They enter with a restrained upward fade and a 75ms stagger; reduced motion shows them immediately. Clicking one sends an ordinary question. New ideas and repeated presets request new suggestions without delaying a cached answer or changing the draft. The separate `/api/followups` endpoint makes one bounded model call per generation, excluding the last 12 displayed questions and recently asked questions; there is no static question-bank fallback. Requests are debounced, cancelled on navigation/context changes, and protected against late results. Contact, New chat, and the 15-question limit hide suggestions. Failures leave the conversation usable; suggestions do not consume conversation turns. Suggestions use approved code-owned profile data, not a live vector database. Nationality stays in request-only Agent background and is excluded from proactive questions; visa, work authorization and sponsorship requirements have not been supplied.
- **Interview Question Bank**: a curated server-only snapshot of 40 English Q/A entries in `lib/agent/interview-bank.snapshot.json`, with stable `qb-01`–`qb-40` IDs, project, topic, Chinese/English matching keywords and factual boundaries. Revision `2026-10-23-question-bank-1` is `release-authorized` by the owner's 2026-10-23 request to release the latest follow-up system; this is not a claim of individual review of all entries. `docs/question-bank-review.md` retains the content and review considerations. Notion remains the editing source but is never queried at runtime; raw pages and Chinese preparation notes are excluded. No extra classifier or vector store. Related Projects/Skills suggestions combine one candidate-inspired question and one distinct conversational extension in the existing single model call; Me can use behavioral entries, while Fun Facts/Contact keep their original directions. Recent IDs/angles and question text reduce repeats.
- **Active project**: the settled leftmost Projects carousel card, or the card focused by keyboard, drives suggestion refresh only while the current Projects overview is active and visible. Historical/inactive carousels do not drive requests. A direct question naming a different project takes precedence for its answer and next suggestions. Browsing does not create messages, spend question quota, or regenerate the cached overview.
- **Interview reference**: optional `projectId` and `questionId` on `/api/chat`; optional `projectId` and `excludeQuestionIds` on `/api/followups`, which retains `questions` and may return aligned `questionIds`. Server validation rejects unknown/cross-project references. The existing resume tool receives at most four lexically matched Q/A entries; a validated click reference ranks first. Answer grounding preserves personal/team ownership, did/would, historical fixes and unmeasured results. Only IDs are persisted in the existing tab session; old sessions/responses remain readable.
- **Answer reveal**: a frontend visual animation applied after the complete API response arrives, not model streaming. Text remains available to selection and assistive technology; reduced-motion preferences show it immediately.
- **Conversation session**: completed turns, successful AI preset markers, and the current draft retained in sessionStorage for the browser tab. Returning through an already answered preset resumes its answer and preserves the draft; an unanswered preset calls the AI. Reload retains the cache; New chat clears it. Failed or interrupted requests never become cached answers. Restored cards use current approved data and skip entrance animations. Old saved cards without an AI preset marker remain readable but are not treated as AI cache entries.
- **Shared profile**: resume facts plus Hongxiang's supplied public interests, self-described ENTP/curiosity, fitness records, LeetCode notes link, Whatnot collaboration and contact details. Public interests include travel, skydiving, skiing, gaming and spending time with friends; no destinations, skill levels or achievements are inferred. MBTI is self-description, not a scientific assessment. The Notion link is supplied by Hongxiang; its page contents and visitor permissions have not been verified. An interest in SaaS companies does not imply business ownership.
- **Portfolio voice**: a first-person AI representation that can discuss general topics while grounding personal claims in the shared profile. It is not a live human chat.
- **Fun Facts Card**: the AI's returned summary appears above Big Three Record, LeetCode notes, Whatnot and Footprints. Big Three Record opens a native modal outside the constrained chat scroller; suggested questions are hidden while it is open, pending follow-up generation is cancelled, and closing restores the original focus and scroll position; LeetCode opens the supplied Notion link. Whatnot and travel Footprints are disabled Coming soon destinations, with no invented account or map. The summary is cached after a successful AI reply. `FUN_FACTS_REVISION` invalidates only outdated Fun Facts summaries when the public profile changes: old text remains readable, and the next preset click generates a new summary once.
