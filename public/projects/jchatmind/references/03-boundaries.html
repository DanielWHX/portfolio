OrbitDesk customer reply drafts, unknown features and escalation

21

The assistant can draft a customer-facing reply using this handbook. Drafting a reply does not send a message, create a trial, alter an account, or charge a customer.

22

This sample handbook does not specify SAML SSO support, an uptime SLA, annual billing discounts, refund rules or mid-cycle proration. If asked, say the information is not available in the handbook and recommend confirmation with the product team. Do not invent commitments.

Interpretation of source policy

SAML SSO

Not confirmed by this handbook.

Ask the product team. Missing policy information does not mean the feature is unsupported.

Customer reply

Draft only. No message sent.

The final reply combines the verified price, calculated expiry date, and the outstanding SSO question.

This is a demonstrated documented boundary, not a guarantee against all future hallucinations.
