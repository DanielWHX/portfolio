OrbitDesk plans, pricing, team sizes, CSV exports and Slack alerts

2

OrbitDesk is a fictional SaaS product used only for this JChatMind portfolio demonstration. These are sample policies, not a real company's offer.

3

OrbitDesk helps small teams track customer requests.

4

The Starter plan costs USD 9 per user per month and supports 1 to 5 users. It includes a shared inbox but does not include CSV exports or Slack alerts.

5

The Team plan costs USD 18 per user per month, requires at least 5 users, and includes a shared inbox, CSV exports and Slack alerts. There is no maximum team size specified in this sample plan.

6

Monthly subscription cost equals the number of users multiplied by the plan's per-user price. Prices exclude taxes; this demo does not calculate taxes. There are no stated discounts or setup fees.

7

When a customer asks about next month's user count, calculate next month's full monthly cost. Mid-cycle proration is not specified.
